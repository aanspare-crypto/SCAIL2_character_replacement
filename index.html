Vesper-9

You wake in crew quarters. The reactor has scrammed, life support is running on reserve, and six of your crewmates are gone. Your only way off is emergency module EM-2, still clamped to the ring. Something else is walking the ring, and it moves through the ducts.

  1. Recover three fuse cells to unseal the reactor bay.
  2. Restart the reactor to power the module bay.
  3. Release both docking clamps, board EM-2 and survive the separation countdown.

Headphones recommended. Sound starts when you wake up.
