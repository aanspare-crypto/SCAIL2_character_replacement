Hollow Creek Colliery · three levels below the surface

Blackdamp

You wake on Nine Seam, the deepest level, where the cutters broke into a cavern that should not exist. The carvings on its walls are older than anyone can explain. The men who went near the black pool came back pale, eyeless and wrong, and they still wear their helmets. They cannot see you. They hear everything. Climb three levels to the cage and get out.

  1. Level 5, Nine Seam. Find the winder handle and ride the staple hoist up.
  2. Level 4, Haulage. Blast the roof fall with three sticks of dynamite and climb the ladderway.
  3. Level 3, Pit bottom. Fuel the hoist generator, ring for the cage and stay alive until it comes.
